A 40% keyboard
with a computer inside.

4×10 CFX low-profile keys, a PocketBeagle 2 running Linux, and a 4.58″ 320×960 bar display — in a translucent neon shell. Drag to orbit. Type — the keys are live.

Case colour

View

SBC

PocketBeagle 2

TI AM6254 quad-core Cortex-A53, 512 MB LPDDR4, on its own 2×18 sockets. It is the USB host for the keyboard and drives the display over 16-bit parallel RGB (DPI) — the official BeagleBoard 3D model is shown here.

Display

4.58″ 320×960 bar

ST7701S IPS bar panel glued under the bezel. RGB565 on VOUT0, 3-wire SPI for init, and a TPS61165 boost driving the 12 V backlight at 39 mA with PWM dimming. Its flex folds 180° under the panel into a 40-pin FPC socket.

Keyboard

bancouver40

The original ChrisChrisLoLo 4×10 CFX-spaced board: ATmega32U4, Kailh Choc hot-swap, QMK/Vial. Its USB-C is gone — the MCU now plugs straight into the PocketBeagle's second USB port, so the stock firmware just works.
